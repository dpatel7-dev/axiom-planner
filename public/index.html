How to find your Veracross calendar URLs
  1. Open the Calendar app on your Mac
  2. In the sidebar, find your school calendars
  3. Two-finger-click on the class schedule calendar → Get Info → copy the URL → paste it on the left
  4. Repeat for the assignments calendar → paste it on the right
  5. Click Sync on each. Classes first, then assignments — the AI will use your classes to match assignments correctly.
