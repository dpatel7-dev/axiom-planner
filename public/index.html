How to find your Veracross calendar URLs
  1. Open the Calendar app on your Mac
  2. Find your school calendars in the sidebar
  3. Two-finger-click the class schedule calendar → Get Info → copy the URL → paste on the left
  4. Repeat for the assignments calendar → paste on the right
  5. Sync classes first, then assignments — the AI will use your classes to match assignments correctly.
